Cut the Ribbon

The São Francisco river transfer project has spanned six governments, and every president wants to be in the inauguration photo. Bring the water stage by stage and hand the scissors to whoever did each part.

How to play: tap the canal pieces to rotate them until water flows from the river to the destination. Then cut the ribbon at the right moment. There are five stages, from 2007 to 2026.
